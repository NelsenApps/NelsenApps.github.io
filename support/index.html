Hanami Sudoku support

You can currently report bugs or ask general game questions through GitHub Issues. You will need a GitHub account. Issues are public, so please do not post sensitive information, personal details, or private privacy requests there.

Your saved progress

Hanami Sudoku saves puzzles, preferences and lifetime scores locally on your device. It currently has no accounts or cross-device synchronization. Clearing app storage or uninstalling may remove locally stored records.
