HANAMI SUDOKU

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026 · Published by Nelsen Apps

This Privacy Policy explains how Hanami Sudoku (the “Game”), published by Nelsen Apps (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you play and how third-party advertising services may process data.

1. Game progress and local storage

Hanami Sudoku saves your current puzzle, progress, difficulty preferences, mistakes, hints, time played, scores, streaks, completed games, and related game statistics on your device. This helps you resume games and review your achievements.

You do not need an account or to submit a name, email address, or login to play. We do not currently provide player accounts or a Nelsen Apps cloud service that synchronizes Sudoku history. Your operating system may back up or restore some locally stored app data according to your device settings.

2. Google AdMob advertising

The Game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show optional rewarded advertisements for extra hints or mistake allowances and occasional full-screen interstitial advertisements after completed puzzles.

Depending on your location, device settings, consent choices, and advertising configuration, Google and participating advertising partners may process information including your IP address, advertising and other device identifiers, approximate location inferred from IP address, device and app information, ad impressions and interactions, and technical or diagnostic information. Such processing supports ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where allowed, personalized advertising.

Google and its advertising partners govern their own collection, use, sharing, and retention of advertising data. Please read Google's Privacy Policy and Google's AdMob privacy information. We may receive aggregated advertising performance information. We do not sell a separate database of players' names, email addresses, or Sudoku records to advertisers.

3. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, the Game uses Google's consent management tools to present advertising choices. When required by the ad provider for your region, an “Advertising privacy choices” control is available through the Game's Settings. You can also adjust relevant device-level and Google account advertising preferences.

Sudoku gameplay can be played without internet connectivity, although advertisements need a connection. An unavailable ad may cause the Game to grant a complimentary mistake continuation under its gameplay rules.

4. How information is used or shared

Locally stored game information is used on your device for gameplay and saved statistics. When advertising is enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK can send advertising-related data to Google and its partners as explained above. The Game does not require access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or precise GPS location to play.

5. Retention and deletion

Locally saved games and preferences remain on your device until cleared, removed, or restored according to your operating system's behavior. Clearing the Game's storage or uninstalling may delete these records; device backups can sometimes restore them. There is no Nelsen Apps player-account database to delete at this time.

Google and its advertising partners handle the retention and deletion of advertising data under their own policies and applicable privacy rights processes.

6. Security

The Game relies on your device's storage protections for saved gameplay data. Advertising services use Google's software and infrastructure. No electronic storage or transmission system can guarantee complete security.

7. Children and families

The Game does not ask players to register or submit contact information for Sudoku gameplay. The advertising SDK may process information as explained above. Parents or guardians with questions about a child's privacy or use of advertising can contact us using the support channel below.

8. International processing

Google and its advertising partners operate in multiple countries; advertising-related information may be processed outside your country of residence, subject to their privacy policies and applicable requirements.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game, its advertising services, or legal requirements change. The current version will remain available here with an updated date.

10. Contact

For questions about privacy or Hanami Sudoku, please visit our support page. It currently offers a public GitHub Issues channel, which requires a GitHub account; never submit private or sensitive information through a public issue. A dedicated support email will be added before the Game's public release.
