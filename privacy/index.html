Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026 · Applies to Nelsen Apps products and services listed below

Nelsen Apps (“we,” “us,” or “our”) develops and publishes mobile games and maintains this website. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be handled when people use our games, applications, and website, including any third-party services integrated into them.

The specific information an app handles depends on its features. The App-specific information section identifies our currently covered games and their particular data practices. If we add a game with different features or data handling, we will update that section or provide a separate notice before making the new app available.

1. Information our apps may handle

Our apps may store information needed for their features, such as game state, progress, saved settings, achievements, scores, or preferences. When data stays on your device, it is used locally to provide those features and may be affected by your operating system's backup or restoration settings.

Some apps may offer online features, account registration, purchases, diagnostics, or other services in the future. We will disclose any such data collection, transmission, or account requirements in the app-specific information below or in a separately linked notice. We do not claim that every Nelsen Apps product handles data identically.

2. Advertising and third-party service providers

Some Nelsen Apps games, including Hanami Sudoku, use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Depending on the app, these can include optional rewarded ads and advertisements shown at natural breaks in gameplay.

When an app uses Google Mobile Ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as IP address, advertising and other device identifiers, approximate location inferred from IP address, app and device information, advertisement impressions and interactions, and technical or diagnostic data. The exact processing can depend on your location, consent choices, device settings, and the configuration of the services.

Advertising-related data may be processed by Google and participating advertising partners for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, ad personalization. We may receive aggregated advertising performance reports. We do not operate a separate service selling players' contact lists or game histories to advertisers.

For details about the third-party provider, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's AdMob privacy information. If a future product uses a different advertising, analytics, or other data-processing service, we will describe it in its app-specific information or notice.

3. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, apps with advertising use consent-management mechanisms, such as Google's User Messaging Platform, to present relevant choices. Some apps may provide an advertising privacy-options control in their Settings when required by the provider. You can also use privacy settings provided by your device, operating system, and Google account.

Some advertising features require an internet connection. Whether the rest of an app works offline depends on that app's features, as described below.

4. How information is used and shared

Information stored locally is used to provide the requested game or app functionality. Where an app integrates a third-party provider, data handled by that provider may be transmitted and processed for the purposes identified above or in the applicable product notice. We aim to limit our own data handling to what is needed for the features we provide and do not use locally stored Sudoku progress for our own external marketing database.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We have not added an independent analytics tracker or advertising script to this website. GitHub may process technical connection information when serving pages; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

5. Data retention and deletion

Data saved only on your device generally stays there until you remove it by clearing app storage, uninstalling the app, or using any deletion controls the app provides. Your device's backup and restore features may retain or restore some records. Where third parties process advertising data, their retention and deletion practices are governed by their policies and applicable law.

If a future Nelsen Apps product stores personal data on our servers or requires an account, we will describe its retention and account/data deletion procedures in the relevant product information. For privacy inquiries, please refer to the contact information below.

6. Security

We use the operating system's protections for data kept on a device, and third-party services use their own systems for data they process. We seek to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information involved, but no electronic storage or transmission system is completely secure.

7. Children and families

Each game's intended audience and applicable age-related advertising settings are determined for that game. Where children's privacy requirements apply, we must configure the game and its service providers accordingly. We do not ask players to submit contact details in Hanami Sudoku gameplay. Parents and guardians can use the contact channel below for privacy questions.

8. International processing

Third-party services such as Google and GitHub operate internationally. Information they process may be handled in countries other than your own, subject to their policies and applicable safeguards.

9. App-specific information

Hanami Sudoku (Android and iOS)

Game data: Hanami Sudoku stores puzzles, progress, difficulty settings, preferences, hints, mistakes, playing time, completed games, streaks, scores, and lifetime statistics locally on the device. No player account, registration, or Nelsen Apps cloud synchronization is currently required. Device backups may retain or restore local data.

Advertising: The game integrates Google Mobile Ads and its consent-management tools. It offers optional rewarded ads for additional hints or mistake allowances and occasional interstitial ads when leaving the completion results screen. Information processed by the SDK is described in Sections 2 and 3 above.

Permissions and connections: The game does not require access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or precise GPS location for gameplay. Puzzles can be played offline; advertising features require a connection.

Deletion: Clearing app data or uninstalling removes locally stored game data subject to device backups. There is no Hanami Sudoku player account or developer-hosted game-history database to delete.

Future Nelsen Apps games will be listed here with their features and any differences in data collection, use, sharing, retention, or deletion. Games with materially different practices may have a supplementary notice linked from this policy.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our products, the service providers they use, or applicable requirements change. Updates will appear at this address with a revised effective date. We will identify relevant app-specific changes where appropriate.

11. Contact

For questions or requests about Nelsen Apps' privacy practices, see our support page. Our current GitHub Issues contact channel is public and requires a GitHub account; do not post personal, confidential, or sensitive information in a public issue. A dedicated private support and privacy contact will be added before public release.
